Getting Started

A simple introduction to installing and opening RiftWii.

Requirements

- A Wii, Wii U or Wii Mini with the Homebrew Channel
- An SD Card that is formatted to FAT32
- A USB Drive that is formatted to FAT32 or WBFS (Optional)
- A computer or device to download RiftWii

Step 1

Download the zip from the Github

Step 2

Copy the contents of the sd-card folder onto the root of your SD Card.

If you have a USB Drive, also copy the contents of usb-drives onto your USB Drive.

Step 3

Copy your mod packs to sd:/riivolution or usb:/riivolution and games to the wbfs or games in your games folder.

Step 4

Start RiftWii from the Homebrew Channel.
